How to connect a Ledger to SyncSwap for a first swap

Learn how to connect Ledger to SyncSwap with MetaMask: add your hardware wallet, choose a supported network, review the quote and approve your first swap.

By Crypto Docket NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 09:04 UTC#5f3f8e4 min read

For a first swap, connect your Ledger to MetaMask, switch to a network SyncSwap supports, then connect MetaMask to the exchange; MetaMask provides the interface and your Ledger approves the transaction.

  • Unlock the Ledger and open its Ethereum app.
  • Add its account to MetaMask using the hardware wallet option.
  • Choose a supported network and have its gas token ready.
  • Review the swap details in MetaMask and approve them on the Ledger.

Adding a Ledger account to MetaMask does not move funds or reveal the Ledger’s private key. The account remains controlled by the hardware wallet, which must approve transactions. For more on connecting Ledger to MetaMask for SyncSwap, the related background explains how that wallet setup fits DeFi swaps.

What should be ready before connecting?

Before you connect, have an unlocked Ledger with its Ethereum app open, MetaMask installed with the Ledger account added, and funds on a supported network. The account needs the token you plan to swap and that network’s native gas token.

Start in MetaMask and choose its option to connect a hardware wallet. Select Ledger, follow the prompts, and choose the Ledger account you want to use. Keep the device connected and unlocked while you work. MetaMask displays the account and prepares transactions; it does not take over the keys.

Then switch MetaMask to a network listed as supported in SyncSwap. A token balance on one network is separate from a balance on another, so confirm the selected network before preparing the trade. Gas is paid on the selected network, and the swap cannot proceed without enough of its native gas token.

Consider two otherwise identical cases: the same Ledger, account, tokens, and gas, but a different network selection. On a network SyncSwap supports, you can connect and prepare a swap. On a network it does not support, that route is unavailable, even though MetaMask still shows the Ledger account. The network choice changes the outcome; having the account connected alone is not enough.

How do you prepare and approve the swap?

Once MetaMask is on a supported network, connect it from SyncSwap and select the Ledger account when prompted. Choose the token to trade and the token to receive, then review the quote and minimum received before submitting. These figures help you assess the proposed exchange and the least amount you would accept under the displayed terms.

When you submit, MetaMask passes the transaction request to the Ledger for approval. Read the details shown in MetaMask and on the device, including the transaction you are authorizing, and approve it on the Ledger only when they match your intent. The Ledger’s approval is what signs the transaction; connecting the account by itself does not authorize a swap.

Signing compatibility can depend on the network, device software, and wallet versions. If the request does not reach the Ledger or the device cannot display the transaction for approval, verify that the Ethereum app is open, the device is connected, and the selected network and software are supported by your setup. Avoid approving a request whose details you cannot review.

After approval, check the transaction status in MetaMask or the selected network’s explorer. A submitted transaction may take time to receive a status update. Once it is confirmed, check the account’s token balance to see the result. If the status remains unclear, use the transaction details to distinguish a pending request from one that has completed.
