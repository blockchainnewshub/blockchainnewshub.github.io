Mercado Bitcoin promises to absorb losses from $38M hack

Mercado Bitcoin says it will absorb about $38 million in losses after a hack across 10 blockchains, while partners froze $11 million of the stolen assets.

By Crypto Docket NewsroomNov 3, 2026 · 21:17 UTC#790bfb3 min read

Brazilian crypto exchange Mercado Bitcoin says it will cover losses of about $38 million after attackers drained its hot wallets across at least 10 blockchains on Monday, Nov. 2, and that customers will not lose funds. The promise shifts the reported cost to the exchange, while the amount taken remains disputed: the company’s figure is below independent on-chain estimates. In its report on Mercado Bitcoin’s pledge, CoinDesk said the exchange also reported that $11 million of assets had been frozen with help from Tether, Circle and Chainalysis.

The incident follows a far larger Binance breach in which more than $6 billion was reported stolen, but the comparison is limited: Mercado Bitcoin’s stated loss is tens of millions, and the exchange says customers will be made whole. That assurance does not establish how much the company will ultimately have to replace, or when normal platform access will return.

How much was taken, and how much was frozen?

Mercado Bitcoin put the loss at about $38 million; CoinDesk summarized its pledge as covering a range of $38 million to $40 million. Blockchain analytics firm Bitquery counted $48.6 million leaving wallets it attributed to the exchange across 10 chains, using the value of assets at the time of transfer. Bitquery also said its total excludes tokens without a market price, and noted that the exchange has not explained how it calculated its lower estimate.

The roughly $11 million frozen is a partial recovery, not the same as a completed reimbursement. CoinDesk credited Tether, Circle and Chainalysis with helping freeze the assets. Bitquery’s on-chain review put the frozen amount at $11.4 million, including freezes by Tether, Circle, Paxos and smaller issuers. Its figures show why recovery depends in part on the kind of asset taken: issuers can freeze some stablecoins and tokenized assets, while bitcoin and ether cannot be frozen by a central issuer.

What does the pledge cover for customers?

The company says customers will not lose funds and that it will absorb the loss itself. That is a commitment about how the exchange intends to account for the incident; the public blockchain record cannot verify the company’s balance sheet or prove that every customer account has been reconciled.

Bitquery’s analysis adds a complication to the exchange’s initial account of a contained attack. It traced activity from customer deposit addresses as well as hot wallets, including 36,042 Ethereum addresses that it says sent balances to the attacker. It reported that this activity continued for about four hours after the exchange said the attack was contained. These findings do not establish the final customer impact, but they make the promised reimbursement and the exchange’s account of its response important to follow.

What should users watch next?

The exchange temporarily suspended access to its platform as a precaution, according to CoinDesk. The next practical signals are when deposits and withdrawals resume, whether Mercado Bitcoin publishes a final accounting of the loss, and whether customers’ balances are restored without deductions. Bitquery said it found no public restart date in its review and cautioned that the attacker may be able to sign for old customer deposit addresses. Until the exchange provides updated instructions, the safety of those addresses remains unresolved.
