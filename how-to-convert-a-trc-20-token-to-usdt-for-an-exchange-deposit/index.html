How to convert a TRC-20 token to USDT for an exchange deposit

To convert a TRC-20 token to USDT before withdrawing to an exchange, swap in TronLink through SunSwap, then send USDT over TRON to its deposit address.

By Crypto Docket NewsroomNov 21, 2026 · 11:36 UTC#94153b4 min read

To convert a TRC-20 token to USDT before sending it to an exchange, swap it through SunSwap from TronLink, then send the USDT to the exchange’s USDT TRC-20 deposit address; the deposit is complete when it appears in your exchange balance. This route replaces a direct transfer of a small token, which the exchange may not list, with USDT on TRON, a network exchanges widely accept for USDT.

SunSwap swaps TRC-20 tokens through liquidity pools on TRON. The swap route and wallet steps are covered in how SunSwap handles swaps and liquidity; here, the key distinction is that your exchange deposit happens after the swap, as a separate transaction.

Prepare the wallet and exchange route

Before swapping, confirm that TronLink holds the token and enough TRX for Energy, and that the exchange accepts USDT deposits on TRON. The token balance alone is not enough: the swap and the outgoing USDT transfer are both contract calls, and each needs network resources.

  1. Start with the token in TronLink. Confirm that the token you intend to exchange is visible in the wallet. Open the exchange’s deposit page for USDT and select the TRC-20 network, which means USDT on TRON. Copy the deposit address shown for that network. An address for another network is not interchangeable; the asset and network you select in the exchange must match the transfer you make from TronLink.
  2. Keep TRX for both transactions. Energy pays for contract execution, not the token being swapped or sent. Staked resources can cover Energy; when you have no staked Energy available, TRX is burned to cover the cost. The amount required can vary with the transaction and the receiving address. In particular, a USDT transfer to an address that holds no USDT costs more Energy, so leave TRX in the wallet after the swap rather than converting the full balance.

Swap the TRC-20 token to USDT

The swap changes your token balance into USDT on TRON, but the first swap for a token generally needs an approval transaction before the swap itself. This adds a confirmation and an Energy cost; it is separate from the later transfer to the exchange.

  1. Connect TronLink and set the pair. Open SunSwap, connect the wallet, and choose the TRC-20 token as the input and USDT as the output. Confirm that the output is the TRON version of USDT. Its contract address is TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8otSzgjLj6t. Check the pair before approving or swapping so you do not select a similarly named token.
  2. Approve, then confirm the swap. For a token’s first swap, TronLink asks you to approve the contract to use that token; review and confirm this transaction. Then review and confirm the swap transaction separately. The approval does not itself exchange the token. Wait for the swap to complete and confirm that USDT appears in TronLink. Retain enough TRX for the transfer that follows.

Send USDT and verify the deposit

The exchange deposit requires a new transfer from TronLink to the copied USDT TRC-20 address. This outgoing call also uses Energy, which is why the TRX reserve matters even after the swap has succeeded.

  1. Send a small test amount, then the rest. In TronLink, choose USDT, paste the exchange’s TRC-20 deposit address, and review the destination and network. Send a small test amount first. After it arrives, send the remaining amount, keeping in mind that this second transfer also needs Energy. A test transfer takes an extra transaction, but lets you confirm the exchange route before moving the rest.
  2. Check the transaction and the exchange balance. Look up the transfer on TRONSCAN and confirm it has completed to the address you copied. Then check the exchange’s USDT balance for the credited deposit. A completed on-chain transfer and an exchange balance entry are the signals that the deposit reached its destination; the token swap alone does not complete the exchange deposit.

The practical trade-off is two stages of execution: the token swap, then the USDT transfer. The first swap may add an approval transaction, and the transfer still needs TRX-backed Energy after the token balance has changed. Before starting, watch the selected network, the amount of TRX left in TronLink, and whether the exchange balance updates after the test and remaining transfers.
