ShibaSwap: How Token Swaps Work

What is shiba swap? It is a decentralized exchange app for swapping tokens, adding liquidity and staking through a wallet, with no account or custodian.

By Crypto Docket NewsroomNov 26, 2026 · 23:34 UTC#c328935 min read

ShibaSwap is a decentralized exchange app: smart contracts and a web interface for swapping tokens, providing liquidity and staking, rather than a token of its own. The shiba swap app is a concrete example of a DEX because trades and liquidity actions run through wallet-to-contract transactions. The project’s Shibarium documentation identifies BONE as Shibarium’s gas token.

A swap sends one token into a liquidity pool, where a smart contract uses the pool’s token reserves to set the exchange rate, then sends the other token to your wallet. In a constant-product market maker, the reserves follow the relationship x·y=k: as the pool gives out one token, its balance falls and the balance of the other rises. There is no order book matching individual buy and sell orders.

Say you want SHIB and have ETH. Connect a wallet such as MetaMask, choose ETH and SHIB as the pair, review the quote, and confirm the transaction. ETH goes into the relevant pool and SHIB comes out to your wallet; on Ethereum, the transaction’s gas is paid in ETH. The quote can change before confirmation because the pool’s reserves set the price.

What the exchange lets holders do

The app’s main uses are swapping, adding liquidity, staking and ecosystem participation. SHIB, LEASH and BONE are tokens used in its ecosystem; none of them is the exchange itself. There is no token called ShibaSwap. A coin promoted under that name is unofficial, so buying a “ShibaSwap token” means buying an asset the project did not issue. That distinction matters: an exchange is software and contracts, while a token is an asset that can be held or transferred.

Dig means supplying two tokens to a pool, typically in a value-balanced pair, in return for liquidity-provider (LP) tokens representing your pool position. Bury means staking tokens. The two actions are not interchangeable: Dig makes assets available for other traders and gives you an LP position, while Bury commits tokens to a staking mechanism. Treating both as simply “earning on your coins” hides the different exposures and ways your assets are used.

Pool pricing also means trade size matters. Hold the pool and token pair constant, then compare a small ETH-for-SHIB trade with a much larger one. The small trade takes less SHIB relative to the pool’s reserves, so its price impact is generally smaller; the large trade changes the reserve ratio more and receives a less favorable price. That is why a quoted price is not necessarily the final average price for a large order.

The exchange launched on Ethereum in July 2021, and its code is forked from SushiSwap’s constant-product AMM. SushiSwap is a separate exchange: shared code does not mean shared pools or the same project. The practical trade-off is familiar DEX access to token swaps and liquidity actions without an account or custodian, alongside the need to manage wallet approvals, transaction details and pool-price movement yourself.

BONE has a role beyond trading: holders can vote on ecosystem proposals, and BONE is used to pay gas on Shibarium. That gives the token governance and network utility in different contexts. Keep the chain in view when planning a transaction, since gas is paid in the network’s designated token.

Related questions

Is shiba swap safe to use?

Safety depends on the contract, the transaction you approve and how you manage your wallet. Review token addresses and transaction details before signing, and be cautious with token approvals that grant spending access. Smart-contract risk and price movement remain relevant even when a DEX transaction is non-custodial.

What wallets work with shiba swap?

A compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask can connect to the app and sign transactions. The wallet holds the assets and authorizes contract calls; the exchange does not create a custodial account for you.

What fees does shiba swap charge?

Separate Ethereum gas from any swap fee associated with a trade. Gas pays for processing the transaction on Ethereum and is denominated in ETH. Check the current transaction details and applicable terms before signing; charges can depend on the transaction and network conditions.

Other questions

How do I buy BONE?

You can acquire BONE through a crypto exchange or a DEX that supports the token. Verify the token and network before sending funds, then account for the wallet and network needed to hold or use it.

What is xSHIB?

xSHIB is a receipt-style token associated with staking SHIB in the ecosystem. It represents a staking position; it is not another name for SHIB itself.

Can I use shiba swap on a phone?

You can use a compatible mobile wallet with a browser or connection feature that supports decentralized apps. Confirm the wallet is connected to the intended network and inspect each transaction before approving it.

What is Shibarium used for?

Shibarium is an Ethereum-linked layer-2 network for transactions and applications in the Shiba Inu ecosystem. BONE is its gas token, so its role there differs from paying gas for an Ethereum transaction in ETH.
