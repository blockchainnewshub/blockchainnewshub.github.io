Berachain bridge: move ETH to Berachain

seo_title: How to use Berachain bridge from Ethereum mainnet

dek: Berachain bridge from Ethereum mainnet: connect MetaMask, deposit ETH, and track the transaction until WETH appears at your Berachain address.

With ETH in MetaMask on Ethereum mainnet, the Berachain bridge lets you deposit it to Berachain and receive WETH at the same 0x address. Use the berachainbridge.app Berachain bridge to move ETH from your wallet into Berachain’s DeFi ecosystem. Because you sign from your own wallet, you choose the transfer and get a source transaction hash to track.

How do you use the Berachain bridge from Ethereum mainnet?

Have ETH in your MetaMask wallet on Ethereum mainnet, add Berachain to MetaMask, and keep extra ETH for Ethereum gas. Berachain mainnet’s chain ID is 80094. The extra ETH is for the transaction cost on Ethereum; the amount you enter as the deposit is not your gas reserve.

This differs from an exchange withdrawal: you sign the deposit yourself and pay Ethereum gas from your wallet. In return, the assets move from your address and the source transaction hash gives you a way to follow that deposit. An exchange withdrawal may feel more familiar, but it means asking the exchange to send funds; this route starts with a transaction you control.

  1. Open the Berachain bridge and connect MetaMask. The visible result should be your wallet connected for the transfer.
  2. Set Ethereum as the source, Berachain as the destination, and ETH as the token. This defines the direction and asset you intend to move.
  3. Enter the amount and review the quote. Make sure you still have extra ETH in the wallet for Ethereum gas. Two otherwise identical transfers can differ here: the wallet with ETH left over for gas can submit its deposit, while the wallet holding only the intended deposit amount may be unable to pay the transaction cost.
  4. Confirm the deposit in MetaMask. This is the Ethereum transaction you sign; once submitted, copy its source transaction hash so you can track the L1 deposit.
  5. Switch MetaMask to Berachain and import the WETH token if it is not already visible. ETH arrives on Berachain as an ERC-20 such as WETH, rather than as native ETH. The task is complete when your WETH balance appears at the same 0x address on Berachain.

A pending Ethereum deposit is still waiting on the source chain; it has not reached the bridge yet. Ethereum finality takes about two epochs, roughly 12.8 minutes, so a short wait can be part of the transfer. If the source transaction is still pending and MetaMask offers Speed Up, use that instead of submitting a fresh deposit. Speed Up replaces the pending transaction using the same nonce. Ethereum can include only one transaction for that nonce, so the replacement does not send a second deposit. Keep the original hash and note the replacement hash if MetaMask provides one.

Related questions

how long does the berachain bridge take from ethereum

Allow for Ethereum finality, which takes about two epochs, or roughly 12.8 minutes. The transfer can take longer if the source transaction remains pending; check the source hash first, and use MetaMask Speed Up if it is still pending rather than starting another deposit.

do i need bera for gas after bridging

Yes. Gas on Berachain is paid in BERA, while ETH from the deposit arrives as WETH. You will need BERA for transactions on Berachain; the WETH balance itself does not pay that gas.

What should you do after the ETH deposit?

How do I get BERA for gas after bridging ETH?

Acquire BERA separately for Berachain transactions. Your bridged ETH arrives as WETH, so it does not replace the BERA needed for gas. Keep some BERA available before trying to make transactions with the WETH.

Can I unwrap WETH to ETH on Berachain?

Yes, through a compatible WETH unwrap function on Berachain. Importing WETH into MetaMask only makes the token balance visible; it does not unwrap it. You need BERA to pay for the unwrap transaction.

How do I bridge my WETH back to Ethereum?

Use a bridge route that supports transfers from Berachain to Ethereum and check the destination asset before signing. The receiving asset may be WETH or ETH depending on the route, so confirm what will arrive rather than assuming the reverse transfer mirrors the deposit.

What if my transfer doesn’t arrive after an hour?

Start with the source transaction hash. If the Ethereum transaction is still pending, use Speed Up in MetaMask; do not create a new deposit while the original nonce is pending. If it is confirmed, check the Berachain address and whether WETH is imported so the balance can display. An hour is well beyond the roughly 12.8 minutes for Ethereum finality, so the source status and destination balance are the useful signals to check next.
